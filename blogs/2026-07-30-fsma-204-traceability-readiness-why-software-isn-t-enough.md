As the food industry prepares for FSMA 204 , I’m hearing a common question from leaders across the supply chain: “If we invest in the right technology, are we ready?” Technology is essential, but it is not the full answer. Food traceability software can process complex data, connect trading partners, and provide a strong framework for managing Critical Tracking Events (CTEs) and Key Data Elements (KDEs) . But purchasing a new platform does not create a working traceability program on its own.
