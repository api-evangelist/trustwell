The food industry , along with the rest of the world, has the ROI of artificial intelligence on the brain. You'll find the topic on the agenda at every major conference and executive board meeting. According to Deloitte , just 6% of organizations said they saw returns on their AI investments within a year, and recent research from PWC found that 74% of the ROI on AI adoption was captured by just 20% of the organizations they surveyed.
