Risk leaves fingerprints across the food supply chain. Strong risk assessment practices help teams identify where those traces cluster around the same supplier, product, facility, or region.They show up in recurring audit findings, delayed corrective actions, rejected shipments, high-risk ingredients, concentrated sourcing, severe weather, transportation disruptions, and recalls elsewhere in the market. What's more, these fingerprints leave traces across different systems and teams.
