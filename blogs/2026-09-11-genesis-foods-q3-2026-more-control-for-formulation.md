This quarter’s Genesis Foods updates give teams more control over how they formulate, review, and manage product information. We've introduced new ways to customize recipe views, organize nutrient reporting, manage allergens, and clean up product data more efficiently. These updates are designed to reduce repetitive tasks and make everyday workflows easier to manage across formulation, nutrition, and labeling teams.
